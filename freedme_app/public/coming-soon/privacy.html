FreeDME is operated by Mobility Exchange, a Non-profit Corporation. This policy describes the information used to provide FreeDME’s mobility-equipment exchange and related communications.

Information we collect

  • Account information: display name, mobile number, ZIP code or town, mobile-verification events, and communication preferences.
  • Equipment and exchanges: listing descriptions and photos, requests, questions and answers, pickup proposals, handoff and return confirmations, and reports submitted to moderators.
  • Identity and location evidence: when requesting equipment, you may be asked to upload a utility bill or government-issued ID. A caregiver provides their own evidence. We extract first name, last name, address and ZIP code for requester review and confirmation. Original documents can contain additional information; avoid uploading unrelated records.
  • Service records: consent events, SMS delivery and opt-out records, security and operational logs, and essential session information.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate members, check service-area eligibility, process listings and requests, screen equipment images, extract document information, support exchanges, handle safety reports, prevent misuse and send communications you request or consent to receive. Document text extraction and requester confirmation do not independently establish document authenticity.

What other members can see

Approved equipment listings, equipment photos, display names and approximate locations may be public. An authorized donor reviewing a request can see the requester’s confirmed name and ZIP code. Source identity documents are not shared with donors or published. Pickup details and item-specific questions and answers are available to the relevant participants and authorized staff where needed to operate the service. Do not put private contact, identity or medical information in public listing text or photos.

Service providers and other disclosures

We use Amazon Web Services for hosting and automated processing, including Rekognition for equipment-photo screening and Textract for document extraction. When live SMS is enabled, Twilio and mobile carriers process phone numbers, verification and notification messages, and delivery or opt-out information. Providers receive information needed to perform their services. Authorized moderators may review retained documents and reports.

We do not sell or share SMS opt-in data or consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. This does not prevent sharing with service providers solely to operate the messaging program. We may disclose information when required by law or as reasonably necessary to investigate misuse or protect people and the service.

Retention and deletion

  • When an exchange ends: For free gifts, the exchange ends when either the donor or recipient confirms the exchange, or seven days after the agreed pickup date and time, whichever occurs first. For loaned equipment, the exchange ends when either the donor or recipient confirms the return of the equipment. These definitions determine retention and archival timing; the passage of seven days does not establish that a gift was actually handed over.
  • Uploaded identity documents: Uploaded identity documents, such as utility bills or government-issued IDs, will be deleted seven days after upload or when the related exchange ends, whichever occurs first. This deletion applies to copies in active systems and backups, not to the separately retained extracted identity details.
  • Extracted identity details: For free gifts, we archive extracted names, addresses and ZIP codes indefinitely, with no scheduled deletion date. For loaned equipment, we archive these extracted details indefinitely once the equipment is returned. Archived identity details remain private; indefinite retention does not grant donors ongoing access.
  • Inactive accounts: An account is considered inactive after three months with no activity. The administrator will review inactive accounts every three months and delete accounts manually following that review. Account inactivity does not trigger automatic deletion.
  • Equipment and transactions: Withdrawn listings, equipment photos, requests, pickup details and completed exchange history will be archived indefinitely for data analysis and reporting. Personal information, including phone numbers and addresses, will be removed before these records are archived. This removal applies to personal information in text, photos and associated metadata, not just dedicated contact fields. This reporting archive is separate from the extracted identity records retained under the gift and loan policy above.
  • SMS and consent records: SMS message bodies, delivery records, consent evidence and opt-out records associated with a transaction will be archived along with that transaction, under the same indefinite retention and personal-information removal policy.
  • Questions and answers: Questions-and-answers conversations will be archived indefinitely when the related exchange ends, unless the user explicitly requests their deletion. An explicit deletion request also applies to backup copies.
  • Moderator reports: Moderator reports, including safety incidents, disputes and investigation records, will be retained indefinitely.
  • Security and audit logs: Security and audit logs, including sign-ins, document-access logs and administrative actions, will be retained indefinitely.
  • Backups: Backups will be retained indefinitely, except for uploaded identity documents and user data subject to an explicit permanent-deletion request. Uploaded identity documents must be removed from backups seven days after upload or when the related exchange ends, whichever occurs first. Archived conversations may remain in backups unless the user explicitly requests deletion.
  • Messages and consent records not associated with a transaction: These records will be deleted when the associated account is deleted.

Photos and free-text content are omitted from reporting archives until an administrator confirms that personal information has been removed.

You may request access, correction or deletion using the privacy contact below. Upon an explicit request to delete your records or account, all data associated with you, including identity details, moderator reports and security and audit logs, will be permanently deleted. This user-requested deletion rule overrides the indefinite retention periods described above and includes copies in backups. The other participant’s records will be preserved after your personal information has been removed. Shared text and images require review before deletion is complete. A closed account reference without your name, contact details or location may remain solely to preserve shared transaction links. We may need to verify that the request is yours before carrying it out.

Your choices

You can review and correct extracted identity details before confirming them. Optional transaction SMS consent is separate from requesting a login code. Reply STOP to end routine texts or disable them in My Account → SMS Preferences. Opting out of SMS does not delete your account or exchange history. View permitted activity and updates on the website.

Cookies and security

FreeDME uses essential session technology for sign-in and security. We restrict access to private records and use technical safeguards; no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not send identity documents through SMS or ordinary support email.

Privacy contact and policy updates

For privacy questions and access, correction or deletion requests, email Mobility Exchange at inquiry@mobilityexchange.org. Do not include identity-document images or verification codes in your email.

Material changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date. This policy is effective November 14, 2026.
