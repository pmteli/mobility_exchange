The FreeDME SMS program is operated by Mobility Exchange, a Non-profit Corporation. These terms cover SMS communications and do not replace equipment-exchange agreements.

Messages you can receive

With your optional consent, FreeDME sends account welcome messages and equipment-transaction notifications: listing review results, requests, approvals or declines, notifications when someone asks a question about equipment or a donor answers a question, pickup proposals and confirmations, and handoff or return reminders. This program does not enroll you in promotional marketing.

Messages may include equipment titles, participant names or towns and links to your account. Private document images, full addresses and question-and-answer text should be reviewed within the authenticated website, not sent in notification texts.

Signing up and verification codes

To enroll in routine notifications, select the optional, initially unchecked SMS-consent box during registration or enable SMS in My Activity. Providing a phone number or requesting a verification code alone does not enroll you in routine notifications. SMS consent is not a condition of donating, lending or requesting equipment.

When you select the sign-in or registration verification button, you request a mobile-verification code for that attempt. These authentication messages are separate from optional transaction notifications. Never share your verification code.

Frequency and charges

Message frequency varies with your account and equipment activity. Message and data rates may apply according to your wireless plan. FreeDME does not charge a separate subscription fee for these texts.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to a routine FreeDME notification to unsubscribe. You may receive a final opt-out confirmation. You can also turn off routine messages in My Activity. Opt-out does not cancel a request, change a handoff arrangement, or delete your account. A carrier-level STOP may require additional steps before texts can resume; changing the website preference alone may not restore delivery.

Help and replies

Reply HELP for assistance when live messaging is enabled, or use the support contact below. Do not rely on SMS replies to approve, decline or confirm an equipment exchange; use the linked website action. The program is not an emergency service.

Delivery and phone-number changes

Message delivery depends on your carrier and network conditions and is not guaranteed. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Keep your phone number current and notify support if you lose access to it. Do not opt in with someone else’s number without authorization.

Privacy and contact

See our Privacy Policy for collection, use, sharing and retention details. SMS consent information is not sold or shared for third-party marketing.

For support, email inquiry@mobilityexchange.org. HELP and STOP responses must be configured and tested before real messages are enabled.
