Test the journeys, step by step

Use the actual FreeDME website to explore donor, recipient and moderator workflows. Choose a journey below, follow the steps and record what happened.

Before you begin

  • Use the reviewer access supplied privately by the FreeDME team. Member sign-in uses your mobile number and SMS code. Staff use their separately provisioned username/email, password and authenticator.
  • Use two consenting participants for donor/recipient tests. Sign out before changing roles, or use separate browser profiles. Staff should use a separate session.
  • Hosted SMS, email and AWS scanning are real. Use only accounts, phone numbers, equipment photos and documents you have permission to use. Do not use the old fictional recording accounts.
  • Prefix coordinated test listings with “TEST”. Use separate items for decline, unavailable and completion paths. Arrange cleanup with the team; do not alter another participant’s exchange.
  • My Activity uses My Equipment, Requests & Handoffs and Questions & Answers. Personal settings are in My Account. No simulated SMS inbox is needed for these tests.

Share your findings

For each journey, choose a result and note the step, expected outcome, actual outcome, device and browser. Notes are saved only in this browser when storage is available; they are not sent automatically. Export feedback and share the downloaded JSON file with the review team. Do not include passwords, verification codes, identity documents or personal addresses.

Journey 01 · Member · FR003

Register and sign in

Before starting: A mobile number you control that is not already registered.

  1. Open Register; enter your display name, mobile number and a supported Bay Area town/ZIP.
  2. Read the policies and choose your SMS notification preference. Submit and enter the code received on your phone.
  3. Check that My Activity opens. Sign out, then sign in using only your mobile number and a new verification code.

Expected result: Registration succeeds only after verification; sign-in does not ask for registration details again.

Journey 02 · Member · FR003/FR007

Check Bay Area eligibility

Before starting: An unregistered number; stop before completing registration if this is only a validation test.

  1. Open Register and enter an outside-area town or ZIP, such as Los Angeles / 90001. Attempt to continue.
  2. Confirm the Bay Area availability message appears.
  3. Change to a supported location such as Fremont / 94536 and try again.

Expected result: Outside-area registration is blocked; a supported location can proceed to verification.

Journey 03 · Donor → Moderator · FR004–006

Add equipment and review photos

Before starting: A donor account, a separate staff reviewer, and two photos of the equipment. Use a harmless unrelated image for the rejection check.

  1. Sign in as donor and open Donate. Complete the required fields and select Free to Keep or Free Loan. Check that Loan Until appears only for a loan.
  2. Upload two equipment images one at a time. Wait for each screening result. Add the harmless unrelated image and inspect its result.
  3. If rejected, check the red REJECTED indicator and remove that image. If unexpectedly accepted, record the finding; do not submit the unrelated image.
  4. Submit with the equipment photos and truthful disclosures. Read the acknowledgement and check My Equipment for PENDING REVIEW.
  5. Sign out. The moderator signs in through Staff sign-in with password and authenticator code, opens Listing Reviews, reviews the item and publishes it. Review Notes is optional.
  6. Return as donor and check the published listing and notification.

Expected result: Accepted photos remain when adding another image. Publication requires moderator action; the listing becomes available after approval.

Journey 04 · Donor / Recipient · FR007/FR034

Browse and edit your equipment

Before starting: An available listing owned by the donor.

  1. Browse Find Equipment and open the listing on desktop and mobile. Check title, photos, labels and disclosures.
  2. As owner, check for Edit Equipment. Make an eligible change and save; reopen to verify persistence.
  3. Open the edit form again, change text, then cancel. Confirm the cancelled change was discarded.
  4. Open the listing as another member and check that the owner Edit Equipment button is absent.

Expected result: Owner editing is available for eligible states only. On mobile, details and donor disclosures precede the images and safety warning.

Journey 05 · Recipient · FR021–022

Scan identity evidence and recover from an error

Before starting: An available item from another donor and your own permitted evidence. AWS scanning is real.

  1. Open the item and select Request this item. Read the evidence and consent instructions.
  2. Try an unreadable document image and wait for scanning to finish. If extraction fails, check that a useful error appears and no request was sent.
  3. Retry with a readable supported document (up to the stated 20 MB limit). Review the extracted name, address and ZIP.
  4. Correct spelling if necessary and check normalized ZIP formatting. Confirm the information and disclosure consent before submitting.

Expected result: A scanning indicator stays visible while processing. The requester can review/correct extracted details; the donor receives a request only after confirmation. Never put document images or personal details in feedback.

Journey 06 · Recipient → Donor → Recipient · FR021–025

Complete a free gift

Before starting: An approved free-gift listing; separate donor and recipient accounts. Complete identity review first.

  1. As recipient, submit the request and check Requests & Handoffs.
  2. Sign out and sign in as donor. Open the incoming request, check the recipient name/ZIP and choose Approve. Propose a public daytime meeting place and time.
  3. Switch to recipient, review the proposal and select Accept suggested handoff.
  4. Confirm both participants see the agreed arrangement and RESERVED state.
  5. After the coordinated test handoff, use the handoff confirmation action. Switch roles and inspect the completed transaction.

Expected result: Approval produces an offer; acceptance reserves the item. Handoff confirmation completes a gift and marks the listing donated. Use an explicitly agreed test item; do not mark an unrelated real exchange complete.

Journey 07 · Donor ↔ Recipient · FR023–025

Negotiate pickup arrangements

Before starting: A separate request approved with a proposed pickup.

  1. As recipient, open the offered transaction and choose Suggest a different day/time or location.
  2. Enter an alternative public Bay Area location and daytime pickup, then submit.
  3. Switch to donor, review and accept the alternative.
  4. Check both participants see the same final time and location.

Expected result: The agreed arrangement reflects the accepted proposal, not the superseded one.

Journey 08 · Donor → Recipient · FR023

Decline a request

Before starting: A separate pending request.

  1. As donor, open the pending request and select Decline.
  2. Switch to recipient and review Requests & Handoffs.
  3. Inspect the decline notification and follow its link.

Expected result: The request shows declined and the notification directs the recipient to a relevant page.

Journey 09 · Donor → Recipient · FR034

Mark requested equipment unavailable

Before starting: A separate pending request for an item reserved for this test.

  1. As donor, open the pending transaction and select Unavailable.
  2. Switch to recipient and inspect the request and notification.
  3. Check Find Equipment to confirm the item cannot be requested as available.

Expected result: The recipient is told the equipment is unavailable; the item is no longer offered as available.

Journey 10 · Recipient ↔ Donor · FR025

Complete a loan and return

Before starting: An approved Free Loan listing with a Loan Until date.

  1. Request the loan, complete evidence review, and agree on pickup using the gift journey steps.
  2. After the coordinated pickup, confirm handoff and inspect the LOANED state.
  3. After the coordinated return, use the available return confirmation action.
  4. Check the completed transaction and the donor listing. If offered, reopen only after confirming equipment availability.

Expected result: Loan pickup and return are separate events. A returned item is not automatically offered again without the required reopening action.

Journey 11 · Recipient → Donor → Recipient · FR009/FR041

Ask and answer a question

Before starting: An available listing belonging to the other participant.

  1. As recipient, use Ask A Question on the equipment page and submit a concise equipment question.
  2. Switch to donor and open Questions & Answers in My Activity. Answer the question.
  3. Switch to recipient and read the answer under the equipment title.
  4. Check consented SMS alerts and their links without including private conversation text in feedback.

Expected result: Question and answer appear under the correct equipment; each action returns to the relevant My Activity tab.

Journey 12 · Donor / Recipient · FR024/FR044–045

Expired request and retry

Before starting: A dedicated pending request; this is a delayed test, not an immediate walkthrough.

  1. Leave the test request unanswered until the displayed pending deadline has passed and the background worker has processed it.
  2. As recipient, inspect the expired request, Request Again and Remove Request actions.
  3. Use Remove Request on an expired test entry and check that it is hidden from your view. Use restore if available.
  4. For a retry, have the donor confirm availability when required, then request again and complete any required identity/consent checks.

Expected result: Expired requests are clearly labeled; retry obeys availability and eligibility checks. Do not change the hosted server clock to accelerate this test.

Journey 13 · Recipient / Donor · FR018/FR027

Pickup follow-up notification

Before starting: A dedicated agreed pickup that remains unconfirmed; allow 24 hours after its scheduled time.

  1. Record the agreed pickup date and time on the transaction.
  2. After 24 hours and worker processing, check the eligible recipient’s SMS for the pickup follow-up.
  3. Follow the message link, sign in if needed and review the related exchange.
  4. Record whether the message and destination are correct and whether duplicate messages appear.

Expected result: The follow-up points to the relevant exchange. Confirm pickup on the website; do not rely on YES/NO SMS replies. The reminder itself must not confirm handoff.

Journey 14 · Moderator · FR043

Review moderator follow-up tasks

Before starting: An assigned follow-up task or a dedicated exchange that reaches its configured follow-up deadline.

  1. Sign in through Staff sign-in and complete authenticator verification.
  2. Open My Activity and inspect available follow-up tasks. Open the related transaction.
  3. For an agreed test task, record contact/outcome notes and resolve it using the displayed controls.
  4. Reopen the transaction and check its actual state.

Expected result: Task resolution records the review outcome. Record a missing task or unexpected state as feedback; do not assume older recordings describe current deadline behavior.

Journey 15 · Member · FR027

Manage SMS and account preferences

Before starting: Your own account and phone; changing a number requires access to both phones.

  1. Open My Account and inspect SMS Preferences, Privacy & Deletion and Change mobile number.
  2. Turn routine SMS notifications off and save. Reload to check the preference remains off; restore your desired setting afterward.
  3. If testing number changes, follow both verification steps using numbers you control. Check that the new number can sign in.
  4. Review the deletion controls without submitting a deletion request unless that disposable account is deliberately being deleted.

Expected result: Preferences persist. Account deletion is a separate deliberate action; routine notification preferences do not replace sign-in verification.
