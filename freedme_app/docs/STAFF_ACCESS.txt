Staff access — FR003 and NFR003; explicit owner decision 15 November 2026

Moderators and administrators sign in at /staff/sign-in with email or username,
a password, and a time-based authenticator code. Member mobile login is unchanged.
There is no public staff registration or default staff password.
All staff roles require this flow, including existing sample moderators. Existing
mobile-authenticated staff sessions are rejected. Do not deploy until the operator
is ready to provision a real staff account; fictional moderator recordings need
regeneration to demonstrate the new login workflow.

Provisioning (server OS root, interactive terminal only):
  cd /home/ubuntu/freedme-rails-demo
  sudo docker compose --project-directory . --env-file .env.db -f deploy/lightsail/compose.yaml exec --user root web bin/rails runner script/staff_account.rb
Choose create, provide email, unique username, first/last name and role.
Administrators also receive the moderator role. Passwords require at least 8 characters
and at most 72 bytes (owner decision, 15 November 2026). Passwords are entered without
terminal echo, not through command arguments, environment variables or source code.
Deliver the initial password privately to the named staff member. On first sign-in,
they scan the locally generated QR code (or enter the fallback setup key) in their
authenticator and verify a code before any staff
access is granted. Provisioning does not send email and does not verify ownership
of the supplied email; the operator must confirm the staff member and address.

Recovery: use the same command and choose reset after verifying the staff member
out of band. This replaces password and authenticator secret and immediately
invalidates staff sessions. The staff member must enroll again. There are no public
password-reset or SMS bypass routes. No default superuser web account is created.

Passwords use bcrypt; authenticator secrets use application encryption. Back up
and protect the existing Rails secret key. Five failed password/code attempts lock
the account for 15 minutes. Codes cannot be reused. Password-to-2FA challenges expire
after 5 minutes, authenticated sessions after 8 hours. Do not record setup keys or
passwords in screenshots or journey recordings. Staff personal accounts should be
separate from their member/donor/recipient accounts.

Deployment: build image, apply db:migrate (web and test environment locally), then
restart web/worker. No seed/reset is required. The migration is additive except it
allows unenrolled MFA credentials during initial setup. Existing legacy staff
credentials must be re-provisioned/reset through the operator process before use.
