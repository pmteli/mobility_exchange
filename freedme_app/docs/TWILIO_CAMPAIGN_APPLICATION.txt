2. CAMPAIGN DESCRIPTION — COPY INTO CONSOLE
Mobility Exchange, a nonprofit corporation, operates FreeDME at www.freedme.org, a community service for giving, lending and receiving free mobility equipment in the San Francisco Bay Area, California. This campaign sends optional account and equipment-transaction notifications to registered users who explicitly opt in: welcome messages, listing-review and publication updates, equipment-request decisions, pickup proposals and confirmations, pickup reminders, and exchange or loan-return updates. Messages direct users to their authenticated FreeDME account to review details and take action. Message frequency depends on each user's activity. This campaign does not send marketing, fundraising, purchased-list or third-party promotional messages. Authentication codes are requested separately and handled through Twilio Verify, outside this notification campaign.

3. HOW USERS CONSENT / MESSAGE FLOW — COPY INTO CONSOLE
Users enter their own mobile number during FreeDME registration and request a verification code to verify possession of that number. Optional routine SMS consent is collected separately through an unchecked checkbox on the registration form; users may also opt in through their authenticated SMS preferences. Users can register and use the service without agreeing to routine SMS notifications. The checkbox identifies FreeDME and Mobility Exchange, describes the message types, states that message frequency varies and message/data rates may apply, and explains STOP and HELP. Privacy Policy and SMS Terms links appear beside the consent disclosure. Routine messages are sent only to active accounts with a recorded consent grant and no recorded STOP. Users may turn off routine SMS in their account preferences or reply STOP. Historical registration, seeded test accounts and requesting an authentication code do not constitute routine notification consent.

The public page https://www.freedme.org/sms-consent.html documents the wording but is a disabled illustration, not an enrollment form. The current hosted registration is at https://demo.freedme.org/register behind the demo reviewer gate. Supply screenshots of the actual registration form and authenticated preferences, showing the unchecked checkbox and adjacent policy links, through Twilio's supported evidence mechanism.
