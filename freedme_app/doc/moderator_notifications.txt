MODERATOR LISTING REVIEW NOTIFICATIONS
Requirements: FR006 listing screening; FR018 notifications; FR027 SMS consent.

A successfully submitted new or edited listing queues one alert per active moderator
(including administrators with the moderator role), per submitted revision. Alerts
are committed in the same database transaction as the submission. Rolled-back
submissions do not create alerts. The journey worker polls every 30 seconds.

Email uses Amazon SES over SMTP with required TLS. Configure both the web and
journey-worker environments, restart them, and run db:migrate:
  EMAIL_PROVIDER=ses
  SES_REGION=us-west-2
  SES_SMTP_DOMAIN=freedme.org
  SES_SMTP_USERNAME=<regional SES SMTP username>
  SES_SMTP_PASSWORD=<regional SES SMTP password>
  MODERATOR_EMAIL_FROM=FreeDME <info@mobilityexchange.org>
  SMS_LINK_BASE_URL=https://www.freedme.org

Keep secrets outside source control. SES SMTP credentials are not ordinary AWS
access keys. Verify the sender identity in the selected SES region. In the SES
sandbox, verify moderator recipient email addresses too, or request production
access. Monitor SES bounce/complaint feedback and update invalid staff addresses.
Official setup: https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ses/latest/dg/send-email-smtp.html

The recipient email is the staff credential's current, non-revoked email contact.
SMS uses the existing Twilio outbox and requires an active, verified mobile contact
and routine-SMS consent. STOP is honored again by the SMS dispatcher. Staff accounts
created without a mobile contact cannot receive SMS until a verified contact and
consent are configured. Staff can use My Account -> Add notification mobile number,
verify the SMS code, then enable SMS Preferences. Existing staff numbers require
administrator assistance to change. Do not infer consent from a moderator role.

Alerts contain a staff-authenticated item-review link, not donor contact details
or user-authored descriptions. Logged-out recipients are routed through staff
password and authenticator sign-in and returned to the item review. Ordinary
members cannot access the review route. Completed or superseded reviews are
suppressed before sending.

Local/test and recording email is simulated; no SMTP call is made. Production email
stays queued until EMAIL_PROVIDER=ses is configured. Simulated ActionMailer messages
are held in memory; they are not a durable email inbox. Alert state remains recorded.
SMS simulation follows the existing SMS_PROVIDER and recording-mode settings.

Operator status command:
  bin/rails moderator_notifications:status

Email 'sent' means SMTP accepted it, not guaranteed inbox delivery. Email errors
are marked failed and logged with error class only. A worker crash after claiming
email can leave 'sending'. Check SES evidence before manually resetting a failed
or sending alert to queued: an uncertain outcome may already have been delivered.
SMS 'enqueued' means handed to SmsDelivery, not delivered; inspect that outbox for
provider state. Missing contacts/consent are recorded rather than blocking donors.
No existing pending listings are backfilled automatically.

Shared mobile numbers (FR003, FR018, FR027)
A staff notification mobile may also be registered to a separate donor/recipient account. Verify the number from the staff account before enabling its SMS preference. Staff access still requires staff credentials and 2FA; member phone sign-in selects only the member account. Consent and inboxes are account-specific. Carrier STOP applies to every account using the number. Multiple staff accounts may use a verified shared notification number, but member registrations remain unique per number.
